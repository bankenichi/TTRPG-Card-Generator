# Privacy policy

TTRPG Card Generator by bankenichi · Updated November 10, 2026

We do not collect, sell, or track your data. There are no accounts, ads, or tracking tools.

Your cards, files, and settings are used on your device. The included SRD library works offline.

If you download from GitHub or another site, that site sees your internet address and the link you request. The same happens when you open a web link. That site's privacy rules apply.

You choose which files to open and where to save or share your cards.

Your data stays until you remove it. On Android, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. On desktop, delete the app folder. Delete saved exports and device backups separately.

Questions? [Contact bankenichi on GitHub](https://github.com/bankenichi/TTRPG-Card-Generator/issues). Please do not post private files there.
